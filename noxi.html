Frequently Asked Questions

What is Noxi AI?

Noxi is an AI-powered study companion designed to help you understand topics and organize your study material.

How can I generate Short Notes?

Open Make Short Notes, paste your study material and select Generate Notes.

Where are my saved notes?

Open Library from the sidebar. Your saved notes are available in My Notes and Short Notes.

Why is Noxi responding slowly?

Response time can depend on server availability, internet connection and AI processing time.

Do I need an account?

Sign in to access your Noxi workspace and account-specific features.

Quick Troubleshooting

AI is not responding?

Check your internet connection and try again.

Saved notes are missing?

Check that you are signed into the correct account.

Page is not updating?

Try refreshing the page.
